Predictions retain their existing modeled-data pipeline.

Data & methodology

PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, SO₂ and O₃ come from Open-Meteo’s CAMS modeled air quality product. CO and NH₃ come exclusively from ESP32 hardware. CPCB pollutant sub-indices are calculated independently using 24-hour means for PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, SO₂ and NH₃ and eight-hour means for CO and O₃. Overall CPCB AQI is the maximum eligible sub-index; at least three pollutants including one particulate pollutant are required. Hardware CO is displayed and averaged in mg/m³; other pollutant concentrations use µg/m³. Raw US AQI is preserved for audit and never used as CPCB AQI.

Historical records are imported from a verified Mandalay workbook. Gaps remain visible. Observations older than three hours are marked stale. This service supports planning and does not replace official health advice.
