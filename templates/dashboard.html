Data & methodology

Current pollutant data comes from Open-Meteo’s CAMS modeled air quality product, rather than a local ground sensor. CPCB pollutant sub-indices are calculated independently using 24-hour means for PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂ and SO₂ and eight-hour means for CO and O₃. Overall CPCB AQI is the maximum eligible sub-index; at least three pollutants including one particulate pollutant are required. All raw pollutant cards use µg/m³; the CO mean is converted to mg/m³ before interpolation. Raw US AQI is preserved for audit and never used as CPCB AQI.

Historical records are imported from a verified Mandalay workbook. Gaps remain visible. Observations older than three hours are marked stale. This service supports planning and does not replace official health advice.
